# L014 A debt-maturity schedule fact is not the balance at its period end

- **Status:** guarded
- **Area:** fundamentals extraction (`debt`) and the FY vintage read in the feature build
- **Symptom:** 868 `fundamentals.periodic_financials` rows were filed before their own `period_end`, and 651 are open entries in the quarantine registry. PMT and its six sister tickers (one CIK) held 51 each. 622 of the 868 hold only a `debt` value, 448 are more than a year early.
- **Root cause:** Filers tag `LongTermDebt` (and `NotesPayable`, `ConvertibleNotesPayable`) on future dates in their maturity schedules; the fact's `end` is the date the debt falls due, not a balance date. The extractor read it as the balance at `period_end`. PMT's 10-Q filed 2021-05-07 reports 139,269,000 with `end` 2022-03-31, while the real balance on 2022-03-31 is 4,737,383,000. Because the feature build shows a vintage from `GREATEST(filed, period_end)`, the 139M was visible from 2022-03-31 until the real filing on 2022-05-06, understating debt about thirtyfold. That is the one direction of error a leverage gate cannot absorb.
- **Detection:** Grouping the quarantined `filed < period_end` rows by ticker found one CIK holding 357 of 651; listing which metric columns those rows carry showed debt alone; the raw companyfacts payload showed `LongTermDebt` with 90 future-dated facts. Measured impact: 220 FY vintages, 130 with debt, 12 securities, 1,796 feature rows dated 2023-01-03 to 2026-10-06, none on the latest date.
- **Guard:** `src/crispdm/fundamentals.py`, `src/crispdm/features.py`, `tests/test_fundamentals_and_predictions.py`
- **Recurrence check:** Run `python -m pytest -q tests/test_fundamentals_and_predictions.py -k "after_it_was_filed or voids_debt"`. The extractor skips a debt-component fact whose period ends after its filing date, and the FY read voids a stored debt filed before its period ends. Only debt is voided: the other metrics on those rows have no comparable evidence and keep the `GREATEST` clamp. The stored rows are left in place and quarantined, not deleted.
